__<h1> Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard </h1>__

## **Project Overview**

An end-to-end interactive Power BI Sales Analysis Dashboard designed to evaluate Adidas retail performance across the United States. This project transforms raw sales data into actionable business intelligence, tracking revenue trends, regional distribution, product category performance, and retail partner contributions.

## **Tools & Techniques Applied**

- **Power Query Editor (ETL)**: Extracted, cleaned, and transformed raw sales records, handled data types, and prepared a clean schema for reporting.

- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Created calculated measures for headline KPIs including Total Sales, Total Profit, Units Sold, Average Price Per Unit, and Average Profit Margin.   

- **Interactive Data Visualization**: Built dynamic cross-filtering visuals, a custom Region-wise Filter slicer, an average reference trend line, and conditional state-level color saturation.   

## **Dashboard Breakdown & Key Insights**

**1. Monthly Sales Trend**

- **Seasonal Peaks**: Sales start slow in Q1—bottoming out in March and April ($25M each)—before surging through the summer to peak in July ($65M) and August ($59M).

- **Holiday Rebound**: Following an autumn dip in October ($40M), revenue climbs sharply during the holiday shopping season to tie the annual high in December ($65M).

**2. Regional & State-Wise Performance**

- **Top Regions**: The West leads all territories with $121.72M (23.39%) in sales, followed closely by the Northeast at $111.74M (21.47%).   

- **Mid-Tier & Emerging Markets**: The Southeast ($103.89M / 19.96%) and Midwest ($102.91M / 19.77%) contribute nearly equal shares, while the South ($80.24M / 15.42%) represents the smallest regional share.   

- **Geographic Mapping**: The interactive State-wise Sales choropleth map highlights high-density revenue hubs across coastal and southeastern states.  

**3. Product Category Performance**

- **Primary Growth Drivers**: Men's Street Footwear is the top-selling category at $131M, followed by Women's Apparel at $105M.  

- **Secondary Categories**: Men's Athletic Footwear ($86M), Men's Apparel ($71M), and Women's Street Footwear ($70M) maintain steady volume, while Women's Athletic Footwear ($58M) has the highest headroom for growth.  

**4. Retailer-Wise Contribution**

- **Top Retail Partners**: Foot Locker ($157M), Sports Direct ($123M), and West Gear ($102M) dominate distribution, accounting for over 73% of total Adidas sales.   

- **Secondary & E-Commerce Channels**: Amazon ($78M) serves as a strong fourth pillar, whereas Kohl's ($39M) and Walmart ($21M) represent smaller retail footprints.
